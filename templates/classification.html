Viva-Voce Academic Review: Class Imbalance & Evaluation Diagnostics

In logistics delay monitoring, accuracy alone is deceptive because predicting the majority class yields high surface accuracy while missing critical delay events. Recall (Sensitivity) measures the proportion of actual delayed orders successfully flagged, while Specificity measures on-time shipments correctly identified.

📖 Viva Questions & Answers: Why SMOTE on Training Only? (Click to expand)

Q: Why must SMOTE never be applied to the test or validation set?

A: The test set must represent the true, unadulterated distribution of reality. If synthetic samples are introduced into the evaluation split, the model is tested on artificial data points rather than genuine future transactions, causing severe evaluation bias and data leakage.

Q: How does SMOTENC differ from regular SMOTE?

A: Regular SMOTE performs Euclidean vector interpolation between k-nearest neighbors. On categorical attributes (e.g. carrier modes or customer regions), vector interpolation produces non-existent fractional states. SMOTENC interpolates continuous dimensions linearly while computing the statistical mode for categorical attributes.
