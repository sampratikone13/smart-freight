What Does This Mean? — Academic & Viva Voce Review

Core concepts of On-Line Analytical Processing (OLAP), dimensional modeling, and cube operations for Lab 9.

ROLL-UP & DRILL-DOWN Hierarchy Traversal

Roll-up climbs the concept hierarchy (e.g., City ➔ State ➔ Country ➔ Market) aggregating measures to broader levels. Drill-down descends into granular detail to isolate specific logistics anomalies.

SLICE & DICE Sub-Cube Selection

Slice extracts a 2D plane by fixing a single dimension member (e.g. Shipping Mode = 'Same Day'). Dice defines a sub-cube by applying multiple filtering predicates across 2+ dimensions simultaneously.

PIVOT MATRIX Cross-Tabulation

Pivot (Rotate) transforms analytical perspectives by re-orienting the multidimensional axes. Placing Market on rows and Shipping Mode on columns reveals that Same Day shipping achieves ~0.48 days consistently across all continents.

OLAP VS OLTP Architectural Distinction

OLTP databases prioritize transactional integrity, ACID guarantees, and 3NF normalization. Our Star-Schema OLAP warehouse pre-indexes surrogate foreign keys to deliver complex aggregations over 59,606 rows in under 20 milliseconds.
