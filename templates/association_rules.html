What Does This Mean? — Academic & Viva Voce Review

Essential market basket formulas and authentic transaction aggregation principles for Lab 8.

SUPPORT Order Itemset Prevalence P(A ∩ B)

Support(A ➔ B) = Count(Order Id with A ∪ B) / N_orders. Measures how frequently items co-occur in the same order basket across all 65,752 real order transactions.

CONFIDENCE Conditional Probability P(B | A)

Confidence(A ➔ B) = Support(A ∪ B) / Support(A). Measures how often category B is added to an order given that category A was already purchased.

LIFT & LEVERAGE Synergy vs. Independence

Lift(A ➔ B) = Confidence / Support(B). Lift > 1.0 proves true positive co-purchase affinity. Leverage = Support(A ∪ B) - [Support(A) * Support(B)] measures difference in joint probability from complete independence.

CONVICTION Directional Dependency Ratio

Conviction(A ➔ B) = [1 - Support(B)] / [1 - Confidence(A ➔ B)]. Directional metric measuring rule sensitivity to independence violation. Higher conviction indicates strong causal rule dependence.
