What Does This Mean? — Academic & Viva Voce Review

Key theoretical concepts, statistical formulas, and logistics implications for Lab 7.

PRIVACY & COHORTS Why Cohort Aggregation?

Direct individual customer identifiers were sanitized from the primary dataset to ensure customer privacy compliance. Aggregating records into 1,487 localized customer cohorts preserves regional logistics behavior while completely safeguarding PII.

WCSS FORMULA Inertia / Within-Cluster Sum of Squares

WCSS = Σ_k Σ_(x ∈ C_k) ||x - μ_k||² measures intra-cluster variance. Lloyd's algorithm minimizes this distance iteratively. The Elbow method detects the inflection point where adding additional clusters yields diminishing returns.

SILHOUETTE Separation vs. Cohesion

s(i) = [b(i) - a(i)] / max(a(i), b(i)) where a(i) is mean intra-cluster distance and b(i) is mean nearest-cluster distance. Ranges from -1 to +1. Values near 1.0 indicate cohesive clusters with distinct boundary margins.

BUSINESS VALUE Logistics Persona Applications

Distinguishing High-Value Customers and Frequent Buyers from Discount-Driven Customers allows carrier operations to prioritize expedited freight allocations, optimize replenishment schedules, and protect shipping margins.
